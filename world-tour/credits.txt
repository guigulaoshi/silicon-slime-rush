Silicon Slime Rush: World Tour — credits and license notices
Version 2.0.0

Asset sources
=============

Everything in Silicon Slime Rush: World Tour was made for the game unless a row below names another source. Each row lists where an asset came from, who made it, its license and what was changed.

Roads, buildings, land use, water and trees of all thirteen routes
  Source: OpenStreetMap <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>
  Author: © OpenStreetMap contributors
  License: ODbL
  Changes: Converted into game geometry by this project's map pipeline; building heights, wall and roof styles filled in per city where the map is silent

Elevation, including distant mountains and the world map on the menu
  Source: Mapzen Terrain Tiles <https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/> via AWS Open Data
  Author: Multiple data providers
  License: Terrain attribution <https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md>
  Changes: Sampled into road and terrain heights, roofs and tree tops taken out in cities; the menu's continents are traced from the same tiles

Road, pavement, ground, bark and rock surfaces (asphalt_02, asphalt_04, concrete_pavers_02, concrete_floor_01, sparse_grass, forrest_ground_01, aerial_ground_rock, aerial_sand, aerial_rocks_02, marble_cliff_03, bark_brown_02)
  Source: Poly Haven <https://polyhaven.com/textures>
  Author: Poly Haven contributors
  License: CC0
  Changes: Colour, normal and roughness maps resized to 1024 (roughness 512) and converted to WebP; pinned by URL and checksum in pipeline/assets/polyhaven.json

Lawn grass (Grass001) and the leaves of every tree crown (LeafSet024)
  Source: ambientCG <https://ambientcg.com/>
  Author: Lennart Demes
  License: CC0
  Changes: The grass is resized and converted; the tree crowns use a leaf mass composed by the pipeline from the single scanned leaves of LeafSet024, turned, sized and tinted per kind of tree

Billboard lettering font
  Source: Google Fonts Noto Sans SC <https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc>
  Author: Google / Adobe
  License: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  Changes: Reduced to the characters the billboards use, with hinting removed

Xiaohongshu logo
  Source: Wikimedia Commons, File:XiaohongshuLOGO.png <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c1/XiaohongshuLOGO.png>
  Author: Xiaohongshu
  License: Marked public domain on Wikimedia Commons; the logo is a trademark of its owner
  Changes: Used unaltered on one billboard, scaled to 160×160

Bilibili logo
  Source: Simple Icons 16.30.0 <https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.30.0/icons/bilibili.svg>
  Author: Bilibili
  License: Icon path data CC0; the logo is a trademark of its owner
  Changes: Rasterized and shown in Bilibili blue (#00AEEC) on a white rounded tile

Social media billboards
  Source: The creator's own share cards for seven platforms
  Author: guigulaoshi
  License: Owned by the creator; used only in this game
  Changes: Redrawn as 1024×512 panels with QR codes and simplified platform marks; no profile photos or account names. Platform names and logos are trademarks of their owners

The 34 landmarks (Tiananmen and the square, the Bund and Lujiazui, the Potala Palace, the Burj Khalifa, the Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia, the Colosseum and its neighbours, the Arc de Triomphe and the Eiffel Tower, the pyramids and the Sphinx, the New York and Sydney landmarks)
  Source: Original Blender models placed on OpenStreetMap outlines
  Author: This project
  License: Owned by this project
  Changes: Built with Blender scripts in assets-src/landmarks from public dimensions and compared part by part with reference photographs from Wikimedia Commons (sources listed in each model's build report); no photographs, text, emblems or logos are in the models

Sandstone pillars and valley mist of Zhangjiajie
  Source: Original procedural geometry and shaders
  Author: This project
  License: Owned by this project
  Changes: Generated by the map pipeline from the elevation data; shapes from published descriptions of the Wulingyuan quartz-sandstone landscape

All vehicles: the original nine, thirteen local vehicles and the city taxi liveries
  Source: Original designs
  Author: This project
  License: Owned by this project
  Changes: Built with Blender scripts: closed bodies, separate wheels and embedded materials; no manufacturer badges or trademarks

Buildings, houses, bridges, street lamps, billboard structures
  Source: Original procedural geometry and textures
  Author: This project
  License: Owned by this project
  Changes: Generated by the map pipeline from OpenStreetMap shapes; wall and roof colours per city estimated from street photographs

Slime, splashes, weather, water, mist and sky effects
  Source: Original shaders and particles
  Author: This project
  License: Owned by this project
  Changes: Generated while the game runs. Glass reflections are baked from the game's own sky, and in the garage from three.js RoomEnvironment (MIT)

Music and sound effects
  Source: Original compositions and sound design
  Author: This project
  License: Owned by this project
  Changes: The menu theme, the three rotating race themes, the Dubai and Amboseli race themes, the shared race rhythm, engines, tires, impacts, slime and interface sounds are all synthesized in the browser; no recordings

Race tunes of eleven routes: Ye Shen Chen (Beijing), Zi Zhu Diao (Shanghai), Ama Lehuo (Lhasa), Masang Shu'er Da Dengtai (Zhangjiajie), Sakura Sakura (Fuji), Üsküdar'a Gider İken (Istanbul), Funiculì, Funiculà (Rome), Auprès de ma blonde (Paris), Lamma Bada Yatathanna (Giza), The Sidewalks of New York (New York), Waltzing Matilda (Sydney)
  Source: Melodies transcribed by this project from: Ye Shen Chen jinghu score "Ye Shen Chen (1)" (reposted at blog.sina.com.cn/s/blog_46db7bad010006lb.html); Zi Zhu Diao dizi score marked "Jiangnan folk music" (maidizi.com/i_17.html); Ama Lehuo numbered-notation score marked "Lhasa, Tibetan", noted down by Chen Yigen; Masang Shu'er Da Dengtai numbered-notation score marked "Hunan folk song" (cangqiang.com.cn/d/895.html); Sakura score on Wikipedia (File:Sakura.song.png); "Uskudara" ABC by Andy Hornby on abcnotation.com, checked against kolaynota.com; Funiculì, Funiculà, Ricordi edition, plate 47127 (Wikimedia Commons, File:Funiculì_Funiculà_original_sheet_music.pdf); Auprès de ma blonde ABC by Bernard Loffet on abcnotation.com; Lamma Bada score on Wikipedia (File:Lamma_bada_yatathanna.jpg); The Sidewalks of New York 1894 sheet music, New York Public Library (Wikimedia Commons, File:Sidewalks_of_New_York_(NYPL_Hades-454371-1822567).jpg); Waltzing Matilda score on Wikipedia (Marie Cowan's setting as printed in 1905)
  Author: Traditional: Ye Shen Chen (Peking-opera tune grown from the kunqu piece "Si Fan"), Zi Zhu Diao (Jiangnan folk song, a basic tune of Shanghai opera), Ama Lehuo (Lhasa nangma), Masang Shu'er Da Dengtai (Tujia folk song of Sangzhi), Sakura Sakura (Edo-period koto song, printed 1888), Üsküdar'a Gider İken (Istanbul türkü), Auprès de ma blonde (French, 1704). Composed: Funiculì, Funiculà by Luigi Denza (d. 1922), Ricordi 1880; Lamma Bada melody credited to the nineteenth-century Egyptian Sheikh Muhammad Abd al-Rahim al-Maslub, recorded by about 1910; The Sidewalks of New York by Charles B. Lawlor (d. 1925) and James W. Blake (d. 1935), 1894; Waltzing Matilda, Christina Macpherson's tune (d. 1936) as set by Marie Cowan (d. 1919), 1903
  License: Public domain: traditional, or the composer dead more than seventy years and the tune published before 1929
  Changes: Only the melody is used, synthesized in the browser on a local instrument over the game's own rhythm and bass; tempo, key and octave set for racing, and each loop cut to the sections named in src/audio/music.ts. No words, arrangements or recordings are used

Game icon
  Source: Vector drawing generated by assets-src/brand/build_icon.py: the slime is a globe, its continents the menu map's land outlines (traced from the terrain tiles above)
  Author: This project
  License: Owned by this project
  Changes: Rasterized by assets-src/brand/rasterize.mjs to transparent PNGs at 64, 180 and 512 pixels for the browser tab, home screen, loading screen, menus and link preview

Menu backgrounds, route previews, vehicle pictures and link preview
  Source: Captured or rendered from the game itself
  Author: This project
  License: Owned by this project
  Changes: Resized and converted to WebP, PNG or JPEG; the link preview adds the game's title

References consulted
====================

These shaped the work above. None of their files, images, recordings or models are included in the game.

- Landmarks: photographs on Wikimedia Commons <https://commons.wikimedia.org/> and published dimensions (Wikipedia, heritage and operator fact sheets), compared against each model; every model's report lists the photographs it was checked against.
- Street style of each city (walls, roofs, lamps): street photographs, used as colour and proportion reference only.
- Slime sounds: Slime Bubble Blowing <https://freesound.org/people/rubberduck9999/sounds/680446/> by rubberduck9999, Water Splash 2 <https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/210427/> by qubodup and Cannon explosion <https://freesound.org/people/SamsterBirdies/sounds/621000/> by SamsterBirdies, all CC0 on Freesound.
- Slime splashes: photographs of liquid splashes, used only as visual reference.
