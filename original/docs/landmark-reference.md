## Santa Clara stadium and Mission Bay arena

The independent GLBs are generated by `assets-src/landmarks/build_stadiums.py`. OSM footprints,
world placement and generic-building exclusion are declared in `pipeline/landmarks.json`.
The models already face geographic north (-Z), with east +X and ground Y=0; runtime yaw is zero.
The stadium keeps the open seating bowl, red tiers, west-side suite tower, field and rooftop solar
canopies. The arena keeps its rounded facade bands and shallow roof. No brand or team marks appear.
Photographic references and source attribution are linked in `ASSETS.md` and the generator header.
